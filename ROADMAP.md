# StyleSaathi Official Product Roadmap

## Master Roadmap Architecture (Phases 1–12)

StyleSaathi is a privacy-first, local-first, culturally authentic wardrobe intelligence platform designed specifically for Indian wardrobes, climates, and occasions.

Below is the officially approved roadmap.

---

### PHASE 1 — Professional Design System & UX Foundation
**Status: COMPLETE**
- Warm editorial visual language (Sindoor, Kumkum, Haldi, Burnished Gold, Raw Silk, Ivory)
- Touch-friendly ergonomic navigation (>=44px touch targets)
- Accessible WCAG AAA high-contrast color hierarchy
- Fast, responsive mobile-first and desktop-capable layouts

---

### PHASE 2 — Wardrobe Intelligence 2.0
**Status: COMPLETE**
- Fabric intelligence (breathability, care, weight, drape)
- Pattern intelligence (solids, floral, bandhani, ikat, stripes, geometric)
- AI confidence scoring and provenance tracking
- Robust local-first IndexedDB persistence with offline sync queue

---

### PHASE 3 — Personal Style Brain & Behavioral Learning
**Status: COMPLETE**
- Privacy-preserving on-device behavioral learning
- Explicit preference modeling + implicit wear/dismiss/reject signal learning
- Category affinities, fabric preferences, silhouette learning
- Evidence-based recommendation explanations grounded in user history

---

### PHASE 4 — India-First Weather & Context Engine
**Status: COMPLETE AFTER THIS CORRECTION GATE**
- Privacy-preserving coarse location resolution across Indian meteorological zones
- Live weather integration via Open-Meteo with local-first cache
- Steadman humidity-aware apparent temperature calculation
- Thermal and monsoon practicality scoring (delicate fabric protection, water-tolerant footwear)
- Truthful weather status hierarchy: Fresh Live → Stale Cached → Weather Unavailable
- 100% deterministic domain scoring and stable candidate ranking

---

### PHASE 5 — Recommendation Engine 2.0
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Advanced combination exploration and candidate diversification
- High-order outfit harmony heuristics
- Occasion-specific capsule generation

---

### PHASE 6 — Indian Fashion Knowledge Graph
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Deep cultural fashion ontologies (regional weaves, festival drapes, ritual dress codes)
- Craftsmanship pairings (chikankari, kalamkari, kanjeevaram, phulkari)

---

### PHASE 7 — Style This Item
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Single-item focal styling workflows
- "How do I wear this piece?" interactive suggestions across occasions

---

### PHASE 8 — Wear Analytics & Style Evolution
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Cost-per-wear tracking and wardrobe utilization analytics
- Closet rotation insights and neglected garment resurfacing

---

### PHASE 9 — Smart Buy 2.0
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Algorithmic wardrobe gap analysis
- Versatility simulation for potential new purchases before buying

---

### PHASE 10 — AI Saathi Multi-Turn Orchestration
**Status: UPCOMING**
- On-device and privacy-first conversational styling companion
- Multi-turn styling queries with full wardrobe context awareness

---

### PHASE 11 — Trend Intelligence & Discovery
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Indian seasonal fashion trends and palette inspiration
- Mapping personal wardrobe pieces to contemporary styling motifs

---

### PHASE 12 — Production Hardening & Android / Google Play
**Status: UPCOMING**
- Android Trusted Web Activity (TWA) packaging
- Offline asset optimization and service worker hardening
- Google Play Store compliance and production deployment
